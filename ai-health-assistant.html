📐 Design System Notes

Key decisions for low-resource, low-literacy environments

🎨
Teal Primary
Culturally neutral, medical-adjacent, high contrast on white backgrounds
🔤
Nunito + Emoji-first
Rounded letterforms aid low-literacy users. Emoji universally understood across languages
📶
Offline-First
Core symptom checking cached locally. No image-heavy assets. Works on 2G
♿
Accessibility
Min 44px touch targets. 4.5:1 contrast ratio. Screen reader labels on all icons
🌍
Multilingual
English, Swahili, French. RTL-ready layout grid. Voice input option planned
🚨
Clear Escalation
Red warnings are impossible to miss. Emergency CTA always visible. Never buried in menus
